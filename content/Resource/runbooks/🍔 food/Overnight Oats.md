# Ingredients
150g | 3/4cup plain fat free greek yogurt
60g | 2/3cup oats.
15g Protein Powder
15g | 1tbsp Peanut butter
1 Banana

125mL | 3/4cup - Water (or Oat Milk)

# Steps
1 - Oats
2 - Yogurt
3 - Protein Powder
4 - Add Water (Or Oat Milk)
5 - Mix
6 - Refrigerate Overnight
7 - Add Peanut butter, banana, pinch of salt

can add honey or maple syrup for extra sweetness
