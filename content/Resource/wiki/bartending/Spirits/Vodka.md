Vodka can be made anywhere in the world and of any raw material.
What makes a vodka, is its [Distillation](Distillation.md) strength.

## Processing
While any raw material can be used to produce a vodka, there are some that are commonly associated with premium-quality vodka.
They are [Grains](Grains.md) (particularly barley, wheat, and rye) and potatoes. 

## Fermentation
Once a sugary liquid is made, [Fermentation](Fermentation.md) takes place.

## Distillation 
Vodka is a [Diluted](Dilution.md) [Neutral Spirit](Neutral%20Spirit.md).

## Post-distillation
These spirits are [Unaged](Unaged%20Spirits.md), reduced to [Bottling Strength](Bottling%20Strength.md). Typically 40% [ABV](ABV.md)
