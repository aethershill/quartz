[[Islay]] [[Whiskey|whisky]] are intensely smoky but rich and full-boded.

In [[Cocktail|cocktails]] the smokiness can overpower other flavors. 
[[Cocktail Codex]] recommends splitting it with a [[Blended Scotch]]. 

Can also be used to in a dasher bottle to add small pops of flavor to cocktails.
Or in an [[atomizer]] to spray atop a cocktail for a savory aroma. [^1]

[^1]: Picked up from Sam Ross's [[neoclassic]] cocktail [[the Penicillin]].
