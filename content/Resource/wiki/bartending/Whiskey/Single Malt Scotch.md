# Musts 
- Made from 100% [[Malting|malted]] [[barley]].
- Distilled in [[Pot Stills]]
- From one distillery

[[Single|Single-Malt]] [[Scotch|scotches]] are twice [distilled](Resource/wiki/bartending/Spirits/Distillation) in copper pot stills. Afterwards it is [[Maturation|aged]] in oak-casks[^2] for, at minimum, 3 years. [^3]

They must be produced by only one distillery, but can be a blend of distillates aged for different numbers of years. 

If the label of a bottle has an age statement (ex. 12-year) that is the youngest whisky in the blend.

# Flavors
Rich aromas, velvety texture, and extremely varied and complex flavor profiles.

` Characterful Spirits `
Flavors:
- Cereal
- Fruits
- Flowers
- Honey
- Fruit cake
- Sweet Spices

Sometimes smokey flavor

The smokey flavor comes from burning peat. 
Peat is burned when the [[Grains]] are heated the end of the the malting process.
The smoke created flavors the grains. 

Note: Only a few Single Malt Scotches have a peat flavor and in some of these, that smoky peat flavor is pronounced.
