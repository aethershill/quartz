Blends used to dominate the Scottish [[Whiskey|whisky]] category. 

Blended scotched are affordable enough for mixing and versatile. 

[[Cocktail Codex]] recommends blends that are distinctive yet not overpowering. Blends with a balance between richness and the spice flavors from [[Maturation|aging]].

The most widely produced, exported, and consumed [[Scotch]].
# Musts
- Be a blend of [[Malt Whiskey|malt whiskies]] and [[Single Grain Whisky|grain whiskies]] from different distilleries.

# Flavors
Some have a medium intensity and relatively simple
Others are more pronounced and complex.

Typically smoother than malt whiskies.
