[[Single]] Grain Whiskies are made using a mix of [[Malting|malted]] [[barley]] and other cereal [[Grains]]. Usually corn or wheat with some malted barley.

[[Distillation|Distilled]] in [[Column Stills]][^1]

Has a light aroma intensity and smooth texture.

Hardly ever sold on its own

[^1]: Not legally bounded to column distillation.
