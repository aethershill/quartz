The main difference between [[Blended Scotch]] and [[Single Malt Scotch]] comes from their ingredients.

[[Single]] Malt Scotches can only be made with 100% [[Malting|malted]] [[barley]] while Blended scotch is a mix of [[Single Grain Whisky]] and various [[Malt Whiskey|single malt whiskies]].

Blended scotch tends to have a more consistent flavor profile. Offering a wider range of flavor notes but less pronounced/distinct regional characteristics.

Single Malt scotch showcases the unique qualities/tastes of the distillery and/or region.
