Aperitivo is a beverage enjoyed before a meal to stimulate the appetite. 
# History
Stems from Roman times when meals were preceded by honey-flavored wines. This, as well, dates back to Ancient Greece.

# Custom
These days, in Italy, Aperitivo has become a social ritual running from the late afternoon to the early evening. 
Beverages are accompanied by:
- Potato chips
- Olives
- Appetizers
- Focaccia
- other finger foods.
